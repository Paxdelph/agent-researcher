# Skill: research_design

- Start from a single central research question.
- Define unit of analysis before metrics.
- Separate descriptive, comparative, and causal claims.
- Specify required data as **thematic files** (events, users, …), not one flat field dump.
- For each file: purpose, row grain, key columns, join keys, and a short **example SQL** extract.
- State what the design will and will not support.
- Prefer the simplest design that answers the question.
- Record unresolved assumptions instead of papering over them.
- When shared company/product `context.md` is present (analyst-level, not per-research), align definitions and platforms with it.
